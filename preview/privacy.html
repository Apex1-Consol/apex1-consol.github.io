What we hold and why

  • Staff users: name, work email, role and an audit trail of actions (for example who signed a hand-over and when). Used to run the system securely and keep a record of decisions.
  • Learners: details needed to enrol, assess and report on learners to the SETA, collected with signed POPIA consent. ID numbers are masked on screen.
  • Practitioners (assessors, facilitators, moderators): details and documents needed to keep their records and SETA registrations up to date.
  • Clients and programmes: contact people and the evidence needed to start, hand over and deliver a programme.

Automatic document checks

Evidence files attached to a programme hand-over are checked automatically to see whether they look like the right kind of document.

  • The check runs on rules inside our own system.
  • Staff may ask for an optional second opinion from a free AI service. It never receives the document itself: only an outline of common document words and dates. Names, ID numbers, email addresses, phone numbers and company names are removed first.
  • We keep only the result (ok or warning), a short reason and how it was checked. We do not store the document's text from the check.
  • The check only advises. A person always reviews the evidence and signs off.

Ava, the built-in assistant

Ava uses a free AI service for some answers and briefs. It is set up to send only counts, dates and general programme facts, never personal information or document contents.

Who we share it with

  • Apex staff, according to their role.
  • The relevant SETA and quality bodies, where the law or accreditation requires it.
  • Service providers that host or support the system: database and file storage (Supabase), sign-in security check (Cloudflare Turnstile), project tracking of signed hand-over records (ClickUp), and the free AI service described above (Google). Security

Access needs a work account and is limited by role. Data is sent encrypted, and actions are logged. If we become aware of a breach, we will notify the Information Regulator and affected people as POPIA requires.

Your rights

You may ask to see the personal information we hold about you, ask us to correct or delete it, or object to how we use it. Contact the Information Officer above, using the objection form (Schedule 2) or the correction and deletion form (Schedule 3) in our PAIA and POPIA Manual, which the Information Officer can provide. If you are not satisfied, you may complain to the Information Regulator (inforegulator.org.za).
